# Creative brief — ex-data-vertical

- **Subject / message:** One data story for a cafe owner: the 8 am rush sells 3.5x the cups of the 2 pm lull, so staff for 8 am.
- **Audience & where it plays:** vertical social reel (9:16), cafe owners (inferred).
- **Format:** vertical (1080x1920), 10.4 s, 30 fps
- **Tone (as numbers):** Swiss/data-poster: light ground, one black ink, one red accent, one condensed display face at 560px for the hero number, 6 bars, at most 8 words per scene.
- **Must include:** 412 cups at 8 am; the 2 pm figure (118) as comparison; hour-by-hour bars.
- **Supplied assets:** none.
- **Data:** SAMPLE DATA invented for this acceptance test (412 / 118 etc. are not real sales figures). A real project must replace them and cite the source.
- **Audio:** none.
- **References:** none.
- **Hard constraints:** keep text out of the vertical platform UI zones (top 250px, bottom 420px, right 120px).
- **Assumptions:** 412/118 = 3.49, shown as 3.5x.
