# Creative brief — ex-editorial

- **Subject / message:** Why there are two tides a day — the Moon raises TWO bulges of water, and a place passes through both every ~12.4 hours.
- **Audience & where it plays:** curious general audience, 16:9 explainer opener (inferred).
- **Format:** horizontal (1920x1080), 12 s, 30 fps
- **Tone (as numbers):** flat colour blocks, one display face at 220px, at most 11 words per scene, one hero move per scene.
- **Must include:** the numbers "2" and "12.4 h"; Moon, Earth, two bulges.
- **Supplied assets:** none — all graphics drawn in SVG/CSS.
- **Audio:** none (silent; a real release would add music).
- **References:** none.
- **Hard constraints:** avoid dark-void / glow / glass looks; left-aligned asymmetric layouts.
- **Assumptions:** 12.4 h is the approximate interval between successive high tides (12 h 25 min).
